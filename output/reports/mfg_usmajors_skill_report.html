Executive Narrative

Across the USMajors / MFG portfolio in FY27 Q3, we tracked 29 use cases spanning 17 accounts, with 22 already CoCo-attached and 7 requiring enablement. The sharpest signal of production adoption comes from Caterpillar, where three Deloitte and EY use cases totaling $2.9M in EACV are backed by 1.39 billion tokens and $24,237 in credit consumption across 39 active days—evidence of sustained, at-scale code generation driving real migration workloads. Meanwhile, Cummins (EY), Cargill (Cognizant), and both Emerson use cases (Squadron) remain non-attached despite clear technical fit around Snowflake AIM, agent-studio, and data quality; these represent $3.6M in combined EACV and the immediate skill-enablement priority for Q4. The partner motion is working where it's instrumented—now we close the gaps where CoCo capabilities map but haven't yet landed.
